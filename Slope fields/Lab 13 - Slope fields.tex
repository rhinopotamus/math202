Consider the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dt} = t-2$. This is a description of the \textbf{slopes} of the solutions.
\begin{enumerate}[a.]
\item I've marked one of the corner points of the grid below. What are the coordinates at that point? 
\item Plug the coordinates at that point into the differential equation to get out a slope. \\
(Hint: The answer is $-1$. Why?)
\item Draw a little tiny tangent line at that point with the slope you got from part b.
\item Repeat this process at each of the corner points 
in the grid given below,
and draw small lines at each point with the slope you find. %% PICK SOME POINTS
\item What do you notice about the slopes at points on the same vertical line?
