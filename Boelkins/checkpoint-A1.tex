\section{Learning target A1, version 1}

Use definite integrals to find the area between two curves. Decide whether to slice horizontally or vertically.

\hrulefill

Consider the finite region bounded by the curves \(x+y^2 = 20\) and \(x - y = 0\). 

\begin{enumerate}[(a)]
	\item Sketch the curves, shade in the region, and draw a representative slice. \\
	(Should your slice be horizontal or vertical?)
	\vfill
	\item Write down an approximate expression for $dA$, the area of your representative slice.
	\vfill
	\item Write down a definite integral that computes the total area of the finite region.
	\vfill
	\item (If you have time) What if it was \(x^2+y = 20\) and \(x-y=0\)? How would your setup be different?
\end{enumerate}
