\section{Learning target A2, version 1}

Use definite integrals to find the arc length of a curve.

\hrulefill

Consider the curve \(y = \cos(x)\) on the interval \([1,3]\).

\begin{enumerate}[(a)]
	\item Use appropriate graphing technology to help you produce a sketch of this portion of the curve. (As always in calculus, make sure you are in radians mode.) 

	\vfill
	\vfill
	
	\item Give me a reasonable guess for the arc length.

	\vfill

	\item Write down a definite integral that computes the arc length of this curve exactly.

	\vfill
	
	\item Use appropriate technology to compute this definite integral. (The integrand probably doesn't have an antiderivative!) Report your answer to at least 6 decimal places. 
	\vfill
\end{enumerate}
